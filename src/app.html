Pros & cons of cramming the core

Works for you
  • Covers the concepts that recur across many questions, so effort compounds.
  • Fits a short runway — roughly 4 hours of drilling instead of 40.
  • Front-loads the memorization items (IR order, volatility, risk math, MFA factors) that are pure free points.
  • Builds the vocabulary you need to eliminate wrong answers on questions you have never seen.
Works against you
  • The 80/20 split is a study heuristic, not a published CompTIA statistic — treat it as a priority order, not a guarantee.
  • Performance-based questions pull from the long tail this list deliberately skips.
  • A 750/900 pass leaves little slack; core-only leaves you near the line, not above it.
  • Recognition beats recall here — the core teaches you to spot patterns, not to reason through novel scenarios.

Use the core to get to competent fast, then spend whatever time is left on full practice sets. Passing this month means core first, breadth second — not core only.
